- Observed failure: core-closure-check showed a concrete Day 2 critique never changes the delivered Plan. Short generic responsibilities and minimal reasoning are not a quality strategy.
- User artifact: complete daily Plan, immutable original, selected concerns, explained changes/declines, independent changed-material checks and exact human approval.
- Baseline: saved negative offline characterization; historical Plan 007 failed delivery. No new matched single-model or comparative-quality claim.
- Hypothesis: a bounded, task-shaped amendment and recheck can turn a consequential critique into an inspectable improvement without rewriting unrelated days.
- Scope: select at most three concerns, one amendment and one distinct-Seat recheck per original Plan. Persist call intent and draft before proceeding; no automatic retry. A failed/interrupted attempt can be dismissed to retain the original. Human edits require this cycle to finish or be dismissed.
- Quality configuration: preserve chosen models; give Plan artifact stages sufficient output space and medium effort on the already recognized GPT-5 family. Other model/provider defaults remain unchanged, not advertised as deep thinking. No model marketplace or new reasoning controls.
- Acceptance: affected-day-only changes; whole-plan constraints; one response/check per selected concern; false criticisms may be declined; unresolved and unselected concerns survive; source-bound persistence/approval; no extra calls after failure; costs/unknown usage disclosed.
- Cost: zero real provider calls during development. Subsequent user-triggered amendment allows at most two calls (12,000 and 6,000 output-token caps), not an invoice cap. A fresh live test requires separate authorization. The old $0.10 is not reused.

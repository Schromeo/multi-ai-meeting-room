# DP-0.3 — Agenda language and meeting trace focus

- **Observed failure:** ordinary meeting proposal, review, targeted response and synthesis prompts are written in English and only the optional unlimited-depth synthesis explicitly mentions matching the objective language. The owner sees meeting speech/output drift from the Agenda language. P2's `SourceAttemptView` is a fourth direct child of `.meeting-workspace`, whose grid declares only three rows; the trace takes space meant for the main speaker/transcript and pushes meeting content away.
- **User artifact:** participant speech and decision prose follow the primary natural language of the submitted Agenda; call evidence remains available on demand, but the speaker, transcript and meeting controls remain the default visual hierarchy.
- **Baseline:** current ordinary-turn system instructions have no Agenda-language rule; the synthesis format requires fixed English machine section labels. Trace is rendered inline between toolbar and content on every meeting with receipts, even while its `<details>` is closed.
- **Smallest hypothesis:** add one common language rule to ordinary Turn Envelope calls, explicitly preserving JSON keys and structural section labels; use Chinese section labels for clearly Chinese objectives while retaining legacy English acceptance. Move trace access into the Meeting toolbar and render its contents as an on-demand overlay outside the grid flow.
- **Expected information gain:** mocked calls can prove all ordinary phases receive the language instruction without paid work. A rendered/component and narrow-viewport check can prove closed traces do not consume the speaker grid row; opening still exposes the same safe receipts.
- **Acceptance:** Chinese and English Agenda fixtures preserve their visible-language direction through proposal, review, targeted debate and synthesis; Chinese memo body and localized headings pass the existing bounded structure checks, legacy English headings remain valid. No validation, budget, retry or receipt persistence regression. Meeting content retains the full grid row with traces closed; trace remains keyboard-operable and readable when opened. `pnpm.cmd check` passes.
- **Cost boundary:** no provider calls, new dependency, record migration, export change or deployment. Keep P2 evidence semantics and privacy unchanged.
- **Stop:** if mocked output still drifts, do not add a repair call or language detector based on paid responses; record the prompt-only limit. If the trace overlay obscures controls at a tested width, adjust the overlay boundary, not the meeting's core grid.

## Local result

- Shared ordinary-turn instructions now ask each Seat to match the Agenda's primary language and script, including targeted debate. For clearly Chinese objectives, Decide and Review synthesis request localized section headings; the format checker continues to accept legacy English headings. Other scripts retain their existing structural headings while prose follows the Agenda instruction.
- Call traces are collapsed in the Meeting toolbar and open as a bounded scrollable overlay. Receipt content, meaning, persistence and export are unchanged. The trace no longer occupies a Meeting grid row.
- `pnpm.cmd check` passes: build, 77/77 offline tests, lint and generated typecheck. Mocked Chinese Decide and Review streams verify language instructions and localized final headings; the targeted-debate fixture verifies the shared instruction, and a rendered component/source check verifies the closed trace and toolbar placement. Zero paid provider calls.
- **Not yet verified:** actual model language compliance, a live Meeting viewport or restored saved Meeting in a browser, and trace-overlay behavior with real receipts at narrow widths. Prompt guidance is not a language guarantee. Stop here rather than adding paid retries or scope expansion.
